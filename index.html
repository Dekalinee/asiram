    About

    Asiram paints slowly, in thin layers, until a picture feels like a room you could sit in.

    Working between oil, gouache and risograph, the studio returns to a handful of forms — the arch, the circle, the horizon — and lets colour do the talking. Work is held in private collections across Europe.
